Delete Your Data

Dawoodi Bohras One · by Mohammad Makkajiwala

Dawoodi Bohras One (package com.makkaji.dawoodibohrasone) has no accounts and does not store your data on our servers. Everything you create in the app is kept only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Option 1 — Delete everything from your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Dawoodi Bohras One.
  2. Tap Storage (or Storage & cache).
  3. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data), then confirm.

Uninstalling the app also deletes all of this data.

This deletes immediately and permanently:
  • Birthdays you added
  • Tasbeeh counters and totals
  • Daily Duas reading progress and bookmarks
  • Settings (theme, azan, Miqaat and birthday reminders, playback speed)
  • Downloaded Library lists, PDFs and cached audio

Option 2 — Advertising and notification data held by Google

The app uses Google AdMob for ads and Firebase Cloud Messaging for announcements. These services link data to your phone's advertising ID or an app message token, not to your name. To remove it:

  • Advertising ID: on your phone open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. Data tied to the old ID can no longer be linked to you.
  • Notification token: uninstalling the app deletes it. Firebase removes unused tokens automatically.

Google keeps advertising and diagnostic data according to its own retention policy; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Location

Your approximate location is used only on your phone to calculate Namaz times, Qibla and Ghari. It is not stored by us or sent to our servers, so there is nothing to delete.
